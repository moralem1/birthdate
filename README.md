# birthdate
Python Birthdate Calculation

The script will calculate the date of birth given a date of death with years, months and days lived.

The practice of carving a date of death followed by the exact age in years, months, 
and days has deep roots in early American and European memorial traditions. 
The short version: it was a standard genealogical convention in Colonial America, 
meant to preserve a person’s full lifespan at a time when birth records 
were sparse or nonexistent.

The custom emerged because many communities lacked formal birth records. 
Churches kept some registers, but they were inconsistent, and civil registration 
was rare before the mid‑19th century. Gravestones therefore served as primary 
vital records, and listing the exact age at death allowed future readers to 
reconstruct a birth date even if it wasn’t written.

Because the birth date was often unknown or only remembered approximately, 
the age was sometimes estimated, which is why genealogists occasionally 
find mismatches between gravestone ages and later‑discovered records.
